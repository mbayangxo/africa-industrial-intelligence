# Packaging analysis: [product / pack]

**Principle:** Better inside and better outside.

## Context and chain of custody
Store/channel, geography, date, price basis, product/lot, image IDs, observer, permissions.
## Product protection and safety
Barrier, seal/tamper evidence, contamination, shelf life evidence, food contact, traceability, warnings.
## Use and logistics
Sizes, opening, dispensing, resealing, carrying, storage, stacking, damage, cold/heat/humidity, route economics.
## Communication and brand
Print quality, typography, language, color, hierarchy, claims, certifications, trust, prestige, differentiation.
## Sustainability and local capability
Material reduction/reuse/recycling, collection reality, local converters/printers, imported dependencies.
## Comparative testing
Imported and local comparators; consistent rubric; consumer/retailer evidence. Do not award preference because a product is local.
