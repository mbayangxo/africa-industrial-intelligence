# ADR-0002: Mission 001 public desk-research launch

**Date:** 2026-09-29  
**Status:** Authorized, then paused due to unavailable public-network access  
**Accountable authority:** Founder  
**Coordinator:** Industrial Intelligence Director agent

## Authorization

Public, legally available desk research is authorized without paid data. Specialist roles may acquire and analyze evidence in relevant languages while preserving original language and locators. Structured provenance and derived claims may enter Git; restricted source artifacts, personal/confidential information, copyrighted documents unnecessarily copied, credentials, and secrets may not.

The first pass establishes the system before opportunities. Blinded independent discovery precedes founder-primed synthesis, and the Skeptic remains independent. Founder retains opportunity-promotion and external-use authority.

## Continuing prohibitions

No external contact, fieldwork, quotation request, purchase, confidential/commercial artifact intake, packaging or factory selection, investment decision, or Kebu change is authorized.

## Execution result

The browser service returned HTTP 401 and the direct HTTPS proxy returned HTTP 403 on repeated attempts across independent specialist workstreams. No source content was acquired. Under the approved stopping rule for unavailable evidence, the cycle paused before baseline, discovery, candidates, or red team could substantively run. This is an environment stop, not research saturation.

## Restart condition

Resume this same authorized desk-research pass when public browsing/network retrieval is functional. External outreach remains separately gated.
