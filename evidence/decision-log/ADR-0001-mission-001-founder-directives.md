# ADR-0001: Mission 001 founder directives

**Date:** 2026-09-29  
**Status:** Accepted for research planning; evidence collection remains unauthorized  
**Accountable authority:** Founder  
**Applies to:** Mission 001 — Senegal rice

## Decision

1. **Geography:** study Senegal nationally and disaggregate wherever evidence permits. Prioritize major producing basins/regions, processing locations, consumption centers, ports/import channels, logistics corridors, and relevant neighboring markets. Never model Senegalese rice as one uniform system.
2. **Time:** use the most recent reliable evidence as the current baseline and seek at least ten historical years where possible. Older evidence may support still-relevant agronomy, infrastructure, behavior, or context only when visibly dated; it must not be presented as current.
3. **Decision order:** first establish system needs and capability gaps; second discover potential businesses, shared infrastructure, Foundry opportunities, and industrial capabilities; third model investments/factories only when the first two are sufficiently evidenced. A rice factory is not a predetermined answer.
4. **Evidence:** material commercial conclusions should use multiple independent sources where feasible, including at least one high-quality primary or authoritative source for important quantitative claims when available. Rare evidence may remain with lower confidence and a stated verification need. Scores cannot validate an opportunity. The Skeptic reviews every promoted opportunity, and material residual risks remain visible.
5. **Fieldwork:** the plan may include the approved participant and observation categories, but nobody may be contacted and nobody may represent the founder or company without separate explicit approval.
6. **Confidentiality:** confidential quotations, personal information, private recordings, credentials, licensed documents, and sensitive commercial material remain outside public/source-controlled Git. Git records only appropriately redacted metadata and provenance.
7. **Currency:** preserve original currency; use XOF as Senegal's primary reporting currency. Useful USD conversions must record rate source, date/period, and nominal/real convention. Preserve historical nominal prices; label inflation-adjusted series explicitly.
8. **Ownership:** never infer ethnicity, ancestry, or identity from names, geography, appearance, or proxies. Identity-based ownership is not an automated screening criterion. Any formal Indigenous-African eligibility definition requires separate founder, legal, and governance review.
9. **Productive African wealth:** report wages, farmer income, local supplier expenditure, employee ownership/economic participation, African investor/shareholder returns, retained locally controlled corporate capital, productive assets, and appropriate taxes/public revenues separately. No combined metric until attribution and double-counting methods are resolved.
10. **Authority:** the founder is mission-launch authority. Agents alone cannot externally publish, finance, present investment advice, or designate an approved project.

## Consequences

The mission plan uses explicit launch and contact gates, geographic and time metadata, evidence confidence, currency lineage, blinded independent discovery, and human promotion approval. Research, outreach, recommendations, and modeling results are outside this decision.

## Review triggers

Revisit if the founder changes mission scope; external representation is authorized; a formal ownership/eligibility policy is proposed; confidential research infrastructure is selected; or an opportunity reaches a promotion gate.
