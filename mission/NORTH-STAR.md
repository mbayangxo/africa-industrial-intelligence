# North Star

## Purpose

Discover and rigorously test what needs to be built, manufactured, processed, financed, distributed, serviced, or improved across African economies.

## Geographic sequence

1. Prove the method in Senegal.
2. Extend it across West Africa and prioritize complementary intra-African trade.
3. Build a continental intelligence graph.

## Outcomes

Increase African industrial capacity and Indigenous African ownership of productive assets; create durable firms, jobs, household wealth, food security, and economic resilience; reduce avoidable import dependence; process more African raw materials in Africa; create globally competitive African-owned brands; expose infrastructure constraints and overlooked profitable services; and inform future Foundry, OpportunityOS, investment, training, and industrial-planning decisions.

African consumers and businesses are the base market. Exports broaden opportunity but must not be the only source of demand. Return by Africans abroad is an invited outcome of compelling opportunity, never a presumption or obligation.

## Decision promise

The system will distinguish evidence from judgment, reveal uncertainty and disagreement, connect industries and by-products, model scale without pretending precision, and attempt to disprove opportunities before promotion.
