# Principles

1. **No invented data.** Unknown is a valid state.
2. **Provenance before persuasion.** Quantitative facts and estimates trace to source records.
3. **Classify every claim.** Use only `FACT`, `ESTIMATE`, `ASSUMPTION`, `SCENARIO`, or `HYPOTHESIS`.
4. **African demand first.** Test domestic and intra-African demand before relying on exports.
5. **Ownership and capability matter.** Track productive assets, skills, supplier depth, and who owns returns.
6. **Better inside and better outside.** Local origin does not excuse weak quality, safety, consistency, brand, or packaging.
7. **Whole-system economics.** Include infrastructure, working capital, maintenance, logistics, standards, and failure modes.
8. **Circular by design.** For every output ask who can use it; for every imported input ask who in Africa can make it; for every asset ask who can share it.
9. **Plural evidence.** Preserve definitions, vintages, and source disagreements.
10. **Independent discovery.** At least 50% of candidates in each independent cycle must not directly derive from founder ideas.
11. **Adversarial promotion.** A Skeptic tries to invalidate material opportunities before promotion.
12. **Human accountability.** Agents propose and document; named humans approve consequential decisions.

## Promotion gates

`candidate → researched → red-teamed → validated → portfolio decision`. Promotion requires explicit evidence coverage, unresolved-risk disclosure, economics ranges, implementation constraints, and decision-log entry. Labels indicate review state, not certainty.
