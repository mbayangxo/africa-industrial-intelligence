# Principles

1. **No invented data.** Unknown is a valid state.
2. **Provenance before persuasion.** Quantitative facts and estimates trace to source records.
3. **Classify every claim.** Use only `FACT`, `ESTIMATE`, `ASSUMPTION`, `SCENARIO`, or `HYPOTHESIS`.
4. **African demand first.** Test domestic and intra-African demand before relying on exports.
5. **Ownership and capability matter.** Track productive assets, skills, supplier depth, and who owns returns.
6. **Better inside and better outside.** Local origin does not excuse weak quality, safety, consistency, brand, or packaging.
7. **Whole-system economics.** Include infrastructure, working capital, maintenance, logistics, standards, distribution, marketing, and failure modes.
8. **Ingenuity before turnkey imports.** For every process or machine, test whether it can be simplified, fabricated locally, assembled from locally serviceable components, adapted from existing equipment, shared across firms, or redesigned for repairability before defaulting to imported turnkey systems. Import only the components that evidence shows are not yet practical to make or source locally.
9. **Circular by design.** For every output ask who can use it; for every imported input ask who in Africa can make it; for every asset ask who can share it.
10. **Distribution is infrastructure.** A product is not industrially successful merely because it can be produced. Map route-to-market, merchant economics, reorder behavior, warehousing, transport, informal and formal retail, foodservice, and cross-border expansion.
11. **Markets are local, architecture can be continental.** Research each country's consumers, cuisines, languages, channels, price sensitivities, regulations, and cultural context independently while designing standards, traceability, packaging systems, data, and logistics so successful products can expand across Africa and diaspora markets without rebuilding the operating system.
12. **Plural evidence.** Preserve definitions, vintages, and source disagreements.
13. **Independent discovery.** At least 50% of candidates in each independent cycle must not directly derive from founder ideas.
14. **Adversarial promotion.** A Skeptic tries to invalidate material opportunities before promotion.
15. **Human accountability.** Agents propose and document; named humans approve consequential decisions.

## Promotion gates

`candidate → researched → red-teamed → validated → portfolio decision`. Promotion requires explicit evidence coverage, unresolved-risk disclosure, economics ranges, implementation constraints, and decision-log entry. Labels indicate review state, not certainty.
