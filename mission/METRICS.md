# Metrics framework

Metrics are defined here; no baseline or target value is asserted.

## Engine health

* Source and claim validation rate; factual quantitative citation coverage.
* Source recency, primary-source share, geographic coverage, and open discrepancies.
* Time from intake to triage and from candidate to disposition.
* Share of independent-cycle candidates not derived from founder ideas (minimum policy: 50%).
* Red-team rejection, revision, and unresolved-risk rates.

## Industrial outcomes

Track capacity installed and utilized, import exposure reduced where competitive, local value addition, African supplier spend, intra-African trade enabled, resilient infrastructure shared, jobs and skills created, and globally competitive products launched. Define denominator, geography, period, currency, and attribution before reporting.

## Productive African wealth created

Report components separately: wages, incremental farmer income, local supplier spending, employee ownership value, African shareholder distributions/value, retained African corporate capital, and productive assets created. Prevent double counting; distinguish stocks from flows, nominal from real values, ownership from location, gross from incremental contribution, and direct from indirect effects. A combined figure is prohibited until a reviewed methodology defines attribution, counterfactual, currency/year, time horizon, and overlap removal.

## Distribution and safeguards

Disaggregate where lawful and ethical by geography, firm size, gender, youth, and ownership. Pair growth metrics with safety, environmental, affordability, labor, displacement, and concentration indicators.
