# Live provider execution

The governed core does not hard-code a model vendor. A live provider must implement the provider interface and may use a model plus web/search tools. Credentials are environment/runtime secrets and never committed.

## Evidence behavior

For consequential findings the runner should seek an evidence portfolio rather than source count:
- market: official/statistical + commercial/market observation;
- process: primary technical/engineering evidence;
- safety: regulator/standard + technical evidence;
- buildability: supplier quotation + field observation.

A source copied by multiple websites is not independent corroboration.

Every candidate thesis should receive a counter-search asking for credible evidence that contradicts, limits or makes it uneconomic.

## Local evidence escalation

When online evidence cannot establish Senegalese cost, wage, margin, throughput, maintenance or customer behavior, create a fieldwork or quotation task. Do not substitute a foreign benchmark without labeling it.

## Multi-provider use

Important packets may be executed by more than one provider. Outputs are compared for claims, sources, contradictions and discoveries. Agreement between models is not evidence; source-backed convergence is useful, while disagreement creates adjudication work.
