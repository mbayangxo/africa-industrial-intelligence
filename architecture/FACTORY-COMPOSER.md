# Factory Composer

Factory Composer converts a researched product/process target into three explicit configurations:

1. minimum viable industrial;
2. locally optimized scale-up;
3. larger industrial.

It composes process steps, machine genomes, utilities, factory zones, labor, quality gates, storage and material flow. Missing inputs remain UNKNOWN and generate evidence, engineering, fieldwork or quotation tasks.

## No turnkey-price shortcut

Costs are built line by line. Allowed bases include supplier quote, invoice, field observation, official tariff, evidenced estimate and engineering estimate. An unknown cost cannot carry a pretend number.

Separate ledgers should cover machinery, freight/duty, installation, civil/building, power, water, lab/QC, safety, packaging setup, pre-opening, raw inventory, packaging inventory, payroll, logistics, marketing, merchant credit, contingency and cash buffer.

## Material conservation

Mass balances must account for inputs across saleable outputs, co-products, moisture, rejects and process losses within a declared tolerance. A co-product receives no economic value merely because mass exists; buyer specification and price evidence remain required.

## Utilization

Theoretical installed capacity is not production. Factory cases model operating days, shifts and utilization separately. Procurement, working capital, downtime, quality and demand constraints can dominate machine capacity.

Expansion thresholds are defined before launch and evaluated using actual utilization, procurement coverage, quality pass rate, reorder, cash cycle and downtime. The system must not move goalposts after observing results.

## Physical flow

The first implementation checks process/zone adjacency and flags obvious cross-traffic/revisit risks. Detailed hygienic design, structural, electrical, fire, environmental and occupational-safety engineering remain professional design tasks before construction.
