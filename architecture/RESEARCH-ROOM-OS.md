# Research Room Operating System

The platform is a governed research room, not a collection of prompts.

## Runtime cycle

1. Frame — Director states decision question, geography, horizon, acceptance criteria and stop rules.
2. Parallel specialist research — bounded packets preserve dissent.
3. Blind discovery — at least three isolated rooms get evidence/geography but not founder candidates, brands, provisional rankings or peer outputs.
4. Cross-examination — contradictions and handoffs become follow-up packets.
5. Synthesis — Director deduplicates candidates, maps dependencies and states what genuinely changed.
6. Red team — Skeptic attacks survivors and defines kill tests.
7. Human decision — no agent makes the consequential build/investment decision.

## Success

A completed packet must contain `new_knowledge`. Paraphrasing the mission or founder idea fails. Outputs expose claims/sources, unknowns, contradictions, handoffs, candidates and disproof tests. Scorecards track evidence freshness, specialist coverage, contradictions opened/resolved, new findings, independent-vs-founder candidates, candidates killed, cross-mission reuse, field/quote requests and decisions changed by evidence.

## Provider-neutral execution

`agents/runtime.py` deliberately separates orchestration from the model provider. Model/API adapters, human researchers and field teams execute the same governed packet contract. This prevents vendor lock-in and lets different models be compared on identical packets.

## Interaction

Handoffs are first-class: Circular finds press cake → Nutrition tests specification → Standards tests safety → Engineer tests treatment → Machinery tests local fabrication → Modeler tests delivered economics. Failed links remain visible.

## Persistent learning

Validated claims, rejected hypotheses, discrepancies, processes, machine components, suppliers and field observations persist across missions with freshness/geography checks. Future operating-company data—actual yield, downtime, productivity, rejects, reorder, merchant margin, acquisition cost and realized co-product value—should feed back so hypotheses can be compared with reality.
