# Mission 002 — Round protocol

## Round 1: parallel evidence

Eleven specialist rooms and three blinded discovery rooms work without a shared provisional conclusion. They must search current 2026 evidence, 2025 actuals and relevant history; older evidence is contextual rather than silently current.

**Exit:** structured outputs exist, novelty is non-empty, source/claim provenance is present, and discovery isolation has not been breached.

## Round 2: cross-examination

The scheduler converts contradictions, unknowns and handoffs into new questions. Agents are explicitly asked to find counterevidence. A disagreement is not resolved by model majority.

Examples:
- Trade sees a large import → Engineer tests technical producibility → Finance tests landed economics.
- Circular proposes cake value → Nutrition + Standards can reject it.
- Machinery proposes local fabrication → Engineer + Finance test tolerance, safety, throughput and lifecycle cost.
- Consumer claims demand → Trade/retail/field evidence tests observed purchases.

**Exit:** material contradictions are resolved, bounded, or converted to fieldwork.

## Round 3: buildability

Only surviving candidates receive startup models. Required: first customer, sourcing, throughput, mass balance, equipment train, local fabrication split, supplier quotes, site/utilities, labor, capex, working capital, unit economics, distribution, merchant economics, marketing, compliance, co-products, 90-day ramp and scale/kill triggers.

At least one fieldwork task and one supplier-quotation task must exist before the OS can call a candidate buildability-ready. Web research alone is insufficient for final local-cost confidence.

## Round 4: red team

Skeptic gets the strongest version of each candidate plus contrary evidence. It attacks supply, demand, safety, utilization, working capital, maintenance, regulation, execution, marketing, distribution and opportunity cost.

Result is **survive / revise / kill**, with unresolved risks. This is a research status, not an investment authorization.

## Human review

The founder receives discoveries, rejected ideas, evidence, unknowns and build cases. The system never silently converts research confidence into permission to spend money.
