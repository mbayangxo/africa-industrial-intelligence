# Mission 002 — Research Room Launch

**Mission:** mission-002  
**State:** SPECIALIST RESEARCH + BLIND DISCOVERY READY

## Director decision question

Across Senegal's nuts, oilseeds, edible oils and connected co-products, what businesses, infrastructure and locally buildable capabilities should be tested or built first, based on current 2024–2026 evidence, whole-system economics and independent discovery?

## Required parallel rooms

The runtime generates eleven specialist packets:
agriculture/value-chain; trade/market; consumer; industrial engineering; machinery/local fabrication; economics/finance; circular/zero-waste; livestock/nutrition; brand/packaging; infrastructure; standards/certification.

It also generates three isolated Independent Discovery rooms. They may use mission geography and validated/public evidence but are denied founder candidate lists, founder brands, provisional rankings and each other's outputs.

## Cross-examination triggers

Contradictions, unsupported quantitative claims, stale evidence, missing current-year data, missing field quotations, unsafe co-product uses, unproven local-fabrication assumptions, route-to-market gaps and unexplained cost inputs automatically become follow-up questions rather than being buried in synthesis.

## Novelty gate

Every completed room must answer: **What did this run teach us that was not already supplied in the mission or founder context?**

A response that only restates groundnut oil, Khoudia, sesame, feed cake, local fabrication or other supplied ideas does not satisfy discovery.

## Buildability gate

Surviving candidates must eventually expose:
customer; first geography; sourcing radius; throughput; material balance; process train; local/modified/imported equipment split; BOM/quotes; site/utilities; staffing/wages/training; capex; working capital; unit economics; quality/compliance; merchant economics; marketing/customer acquisition; co-product disposition; 90-day start plan; scale triggers; kill conditions.

Unknown values remain UNKNOWN and generate acquisition tasks.

## Human gate

The research room may discover, challenge, model, reject and synthesize. It does not authorize investment, solicitation, construction or launch.
