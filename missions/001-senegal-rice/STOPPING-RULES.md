# Mission 001 stopping rules

Stopping means pause, reject, narrow, or conclude with uncertainty; it never authorizes filling gaps by invention.

## Whole-mission pause conditions

Pause and escalate to the founder for scope, budget, timeline, safety, legal/ethical, secure-storage, or external-representation changes; material inability to preserve provenance; compromised independent discovery; systemic definition/identity errors; or evidence that collection could harm participants. Unauthorized external contact stops immediately.

## Workstream saturation/closure

A workstream may close when its decision questions have an evidence map, searched source classes and field coverage are logged, contradictions and geographic/time gaps are explicit, marginal collection is unlikely to change the decision within the approved time/budget, and the reviewer accepts limitations. It may close as `insufficient evidence`; completeness is not required to report uncertainty.

## Candidate kill or defer rules

Reject or defer a candidate when credible evidence establishes any material condition that cannot reasonably be mitigated within scope: absent/insufficient paying demand; noncompetitive delivered economics; unsafe or unlawful pathway; infeasible feedstock/quality/seasonality; critical infrastructure or capability with no plausible path; working-capital/scale mismatch; superior lower-cost intervention; unacceptable harm; or opportunity cost that dominates. Record evidence, assumptions, reversibility, and what could reopen review.

## Modeling stop rules

Do not start or stop a model when system/candidate gates fail; key inputs have no defensible range; units/product forms/currencies cannot be reconciled; mass balance fails; modeled capacity contradicts addressable demand without explanation; or outputs are being treated as forecasts. Sensitivity analysis cannot rescue structurally unsupported inputs.

## Fieldwork stop rules

Stop the relevant activity for missing/withdrawn consent, unexpected personal/safety risk, disease/food-safety hazard beyond protocol, unauthorized recording/photography, data breach/loss, coercion, misrepresentation, required permission failure, instrument defect that invalidates answers, or saturation/precision reached under the approved design. Preserve incident and exclusion logs securely.

## Mission completion condition

The Director may propose completion when the six controlling questions each have a supported answer or explicit unresolved finding; deliverables and provenance validate; independent discovery is complete or transparently failed; the Skeptic has reviewed promoted candidates; do-not-build findings are recorded; and the founder accepts the limitations and disposition. Completion does not validate any opportunity automatically.
