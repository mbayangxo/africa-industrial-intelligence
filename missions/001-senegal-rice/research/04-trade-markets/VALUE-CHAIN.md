# Trade and market channel overlay — evidence collection frame

**Status:** no Senegal-specific market-channel findings asserted.

Future evidence should connect import entry and domestic production to storage,
wholesale, retail and food-service channels without assuming that formal trade
records reveal ownership, margins, product quality or consumer preference.

For every evidenced channel, record geography, product definition, date,
customer segment, price basis, packaging/format, availability evidence,
logistics handoffs and the distinction between observation and interpretation.
Conflicting descriptions of the same channel must be retained for discrepancy
review.
