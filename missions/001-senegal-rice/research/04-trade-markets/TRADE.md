# Senegal rice import, export and regional-flow map — desk-research status

**Status:** evidence acquisition blocked; no trade values, volumes, origins or
concentration findings asserted.

## Acquisition design

The planned series requires reporter, partner, flow, HS revision and code,
quantity unit, net weight, trade value, valuation basis, year, and revision
status. Rice types should only be separated at the level supported by the
reported commodity code. Formal customs trade must not be presented as a full
measure of informal cross-border flows or domestic availability.

| Question | Preferred evidence | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| Annual import volume and value | Senegal customs/national statistics, cross-checked with UN Comtrade | Unavailable |
| Product composition | Commodity-code observations with nomenclature version | Unavailable |
| Origin and concentration | Partner-level records on a consistent basis | Unavailable |
| Import seasonality | Monthly customs observations where published | Unavailable |
| Formal exports/re-exports | Flow records plus documented re-export treatment | Unavailable |
| Neighboring-market flows | Official regional evidence plus explicit informal-flow limitations | Unavailable |
| Prices | Unit values clearly distinguished from transaction or retail prices | Unavailable |

## Required cautions

Future analysis must test mirror-data discrepancies, missing quantity fields,
suppression, revisions, partner attribution, valuation differences, and whether
reported quantities use net weight or a supplementary unit. Unit value is not
automatically a market price.
