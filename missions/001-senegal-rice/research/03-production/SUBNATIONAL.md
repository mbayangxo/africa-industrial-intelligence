# Subnational production map — desk-research status

**Status:** evidence acquisition blocked; no regional production values or
rankings asserted.

## Required unit of observation

The eventual map should retain the source's own administrative or agroecological
unit, production season, crop definition, and paddy/milled basis. It must not
merge unlike regional, basin or project-area observations without a documented
crosswalk.

| Required layer | Coverage | Minimum acceptance condition |
|---|---|---|
| Production quantity | Unavailable | Region/basin, season, unit, crop basis and locator |
| Harvested area | Unavailable | Geography and definition compatible with production |
| Yield | Unavailable | Reported or transparently derived; denominator documented |
| Irrigated/rain-fed split | Unavailable | Production-system definitions and geography |
| Crop calendar | Unavailable | System- and geography-specific source |
| Processing locations | Unavailable | Named facility/location evidence with date |
| Consumption centers | Unavailable | Survey, channel or distribution evidence |
| Ports and corridors | Unavailable | Trade/logistics evidence, not map inference alone |

## Comparability controls

Future observations must flag boundary changes, incomplete geographic coverage,
different crop years, forecast versus final estimates, and paddy versus milled
rice. Missing values must remain missing rather than being treated as zero.
