# Mission 001 unresolved questions and risks

These items are not findings about Senegal's rice economy. They are planning decisions or risks to resolve after plan review and, where indicated, before launch.

## Launch-blocking decisions

1. Name the Mission Director, human reviewers, data controller, field lead, and agent assignments.
2. Approve a time box, budget envelope, procurement rules, and priority trade-offs.
3. Select the encrypted external-artifact platform, hosting/legal jurisdiction, access roles, retention/deletion schedule, backup, incident response, and secrets manager.
4. Specify whether launch approval includes only desk acquisition or any external outreach; later name authorized representatives and scripts.
5. Approve the initial source acquisition list and rules for paid/licensed material.
6. Approve languages, ethics/legal review path, consent/compensation policy, participant safeguarding, and institutional permissions before fieldwork.

## Method decisions to resolve early

* Rice taxonomy: paddy versus milled forms, broken rice, varieties/grades, conversion factors and HS/version mapping.
* Geographic priority framework and treatment of changing administrative/basin boundaries.
* Crop year, marketing year, calendar year and season alignment.
* Definitions of consumption, apparent consumption, loss, capacity, utilization, local product, imported product, processor, and farm categories.
* Price basis, quality adjustment, inflation index, FX source/convention, and uncertainty display.
* Materiality thresholds for triangulation, evidence review, opportunity promotion and model sensitivity.
* Consumer and outlet sampling frames, product comparators, safe preparation/testing, and separation of origin cues.
* Rules for documenting organizational ownership/control without inferring personal identity.

## Known research risks

National aggregation may conceal distinct systems; recent figures may be provisional; sources may use incompatible product forms or years; informal activity and losses may be poorly observed; mirror trade may differ; interview access may bias samples; prices may be seasonal/quality-specific; capacity claims may confuse nameplate and usable throughput; supplier quotations may be strategic or incomparable; and rare technical evidence may resist triangulation.

Consumer reports may diverge from purchase behavior; origin may be mislabeled or unknown; sensory tests may be unsafe or confounded; field presence can create expectations; translation and enumerator effects can distort responses. Commercial confidentiality may prevent reproducibility. Political, regulatory, weather, hydrological, energy, FX and market conditions can change during the mission.

## Governance risks

Premature factory framing, brand selection, national generalization, automated ownership classification, combined wealth claims, score-driven promotion, disclosure of restricted material, and agent representation as founder authority are prohibited but require active review. The plan itself does not eliminate these risks.
