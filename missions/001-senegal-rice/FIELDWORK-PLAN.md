# Mission 001 fieldwork plan

**Status:** DESIGN ONLY. No contact, recruitment, observation, photography, quotation request, or external representation is authorized.

## Objectives and sequencing

Fieldwork fills decision-critical gaps that documentary evidence cannot answer; it does not decorate the report. Begin only after desk-research gap mapping, approved instruments, ethics/privacy review, and explicit founder authorization specifying activities and who may represent the mission. Pilot instruments before broader collection.

## Proposed modules

| Module | Potential participants/sites | Purpose | Example outputs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Production | Farmers, producer groups, agricultural researchers | Systems, seasons, inputs, water, harvest/loss, quality, selling and constraints | Pseudonymized coded observations; never population estimates from convenience interviews |
| Processing | Millers/processors, drying/storage operators, labs | Flow, quality, capacity/utilization basis, downtime, utilities, maintenance and material streams | Process observations, evidence-linked ranges, bottlenecks |
| Market/logistics | Distributors, wholesalers, retailers, traditional markets, supermarkets, logistics providers | Routes, handling, service levels, prices, assortment, availability, purchasing and loss | Time/location-stamped observations and price basis |
| Consumer | Appropriately sampled consumers | Choice, cooking/use, repeat behavior, quality versus packaging perception | Segment-aware interviews/tests with limitations |
| Technical/commercial | Machinery/packaging suppliers and service firms | Specifications, support, quotations, spares and local-capability hypotheses | Restricted quotation metadata and comparable duty points |
| Institutions | Relevant government, industry and research institutions | Definitions, programs, records, regulations and disagreements | Attributed or anonymized notes per consent |

## Sampling and geographic design

Before launch, use documentary mapping to select contrasting producing systems/basins, processor types/scales, consumption centers, import/logistics nodes, and channels. Define sampling frame, inclusion/exclusion, recruitment, target saturation or precision, language, season/timing, and nonresponse tracking for each module. Purposeful qualitative samples support mechanisms, not prevalence. Quantitative claims require probability or otherwise defensible sampling and stated uncertainty.

## Consumer/product/packaging protocol

Separate: (1) observable pack/shelf audit; (2) blind, coded product preparation and sensory/functional comparison under safe standardized conditions; and (3) identified packaging/brand evaluation. Randomize order, standardize cooking where applicable, record product/lot and chain of custody, avoid leading local/imported framing, and separate stated preference from observed purchase/repeat behavior. No brand-name winner is selected.

## Price and observation protocol

Record date/time, exact location at an appropriately safe precision, channel, product/brand, origin claim as displayed (not independently verified), variety/grade/form, pack/lot, quantity/unit, posted versus transacted price, taxes/fees, promotion, stock status, observer, and image/receipt link where permitted. Preserve XOF; record other currencies as observed. Repeated observations must distinguish the same outlet from independent outlets.

## Ethics, safety, and representation

Use understandable consent scripts, voluntary participation, withdrawal, minimal data, age safeguards, culturally and linguistically appropriate instruments, fair non-coercive compensation, do-no-harm procedures, and enumerator safety. Do not promise investment, procurement, services, government affiliation, confidentiality beyond the system's ability, or benefit from participation. External scripts identify the approved entity and purpose exactly; field staff do not improvise founder/company claims.

Photography requires site and, where applicable, subject permission. Avoid faces and identifying data unless necessary and explicitly consented. Food handling/sensory work requires safety review. Report suspected hazards through an approved escalation path; agents do not issue professional advice.

## Data handling and quality assurance

Use pseudonymous respondent IDs; store identity keys/consent separately in the approved restricted system. Encrypt devices and transfer promptly; no raw field data in Git. Train enumerators, pilot/translate/back-translate instruments, keep versioned forms, record refusals/missingness, run range and duplicate checks, verify a defined subset, and preserve corrections/audit logs. Human reviewers approve coding and any quotation.

## Fieldwork launch checklist

Founder authorizes named activities and representatives; accountable field lead; locations/dates; reviewed sampling and instruments; languages; ethics/legal/safety assessment; consent and compensation; secure platform/access/retention; incident/withdrawal plan; budget/logistics; required institutional permissions; and stop authority. Any missing item blocks the relevant module.
