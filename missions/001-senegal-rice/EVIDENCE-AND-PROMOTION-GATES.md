# Mission 001 evidence and promotion gates

Gates are minimum conditions, not scores. A high aggregate score never overrides a failed gate. `Unknown`, `disputed`, and `not applicable` are explicit states.

## Confidence dimensions

Assess source authority/independence, method transparency, definition fit, geography, time relevance, reproducibility, triangulation, and directness to the decision. Confidence is claim-specific. A rare unique source may be retained at low/medium confidence with a named verification action rather than discarded.

## Stage gates

### Gate 0 — launch

Founder explicitly approves research start; Director/reviewers, scope, budget/time box, secure storage, acquisition priorities, and any authorized outreach are named. Otherwise remain `NOT STARTED`.

### Gate 1 — baseline usable

Core definitions and conversion rules are versioned; national and priority subnational coverage/gaps are mapped; latest baseline and historical series availability are assessed; important sources are provenance-checked; contradictions and old-data caveats are visible. Passing does not imply the system is fully known.

### Gate 2 — candidate eligible for research

A candidate has a specific customer/need and geography, discovery mode and origin, supporting claim IDs, competing explanations/substitutes, infrastructure/standards dependencies, explicit unknowns, and falsification criteria. It is still a hypothesis, not a recommendation.

### Gate 3 — material commercial conclusion eligible

Prefer multiple independent sources where feasible and at least one high-quality primary/authoritative source for important quantitative claims when available. Define market/technical terms, period/geography, units/currency, method and uncertainty. Demonstrate customer behavior or verification need, plausible supply/operations, regulation/safety, competition, infrastructure, implementation path, downside, and why missing evidence does not invalidate the conclusion. Untriangulated rare evidence is labeled and cannot be described as established.

### Gate 4 — scenario/model eligible

Gates 1–3 support the question; model has an evidence-linked input ledger; original currencies and FX lineage; explicit assumptions/scenarios; material/mass and capacity-demand checks; utilization/ramp, working capital, utilities, maintenance, logistics, sensitivities and downside; wealth components separate; no false precision. Factory modeling cannot precede adequate system and opportunity evidence.

### Gate 5 — red-team promotion

An independent Skeptic reviews source coverage, demand/willingness to pay, agronomy/supply, quality, safety/regulation, competition, economics, working capital, logistics, infrastructure, scale, execution, ownership/wealth assertions, circular uses, alternative interventions and opportunity cost. Verdict is `fail`, `revise`, or `pass with residual risks`; all material residual risks remain attached.

### Gate 6 — validated for internal decision

The founder/human authority reviews the complete evidence and red-team record, confirms mandatory gates, accepts residual risk, and records a decision. `VALIDATED` means suitable for the stated internal decision and date—not guaranteed, externally approved, financed, or investment advice. External publication, financing or approved-project status requires a separate recorded human authorization.

## Automatic blockers

Invented/untraceable data; unresolved identity or unit errors; confidential evidence used beyond rights; personal identity inference; undisclosed material conflict; absent Skeptic review; hidden fatal safety/regulatory concern; model presented as observed fact; or agent-only external approval.
