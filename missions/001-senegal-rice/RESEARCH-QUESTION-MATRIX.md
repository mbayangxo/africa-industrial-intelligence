# Mission 001 research question matrix

Each answer must link atomic claim IDs and show geography, period, method, classification, confidence, contradictions, and missing evidence.

| Control question | Decision-relevant subquestions | Evidence sought | Disaggregation | Primary handoff |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| What is happening? | What are production, area/yield, consumption, trade, prices, varieties, seasons, actors, capacity and flows? | Comparable official series, customs/trade records, market/processor records, field verification | Basin/region, season, product form/grade, channel, actor | Baseline → all workstreams |
| Why is it happening? | Which agronomic, water, input, loss, quality, finance, logistics, distribution, consumer and trust mechanisms explain outcomes? | Technical/operational evidence, causal studies, interviews/observations designed to test rival explanations | Production system, location, actor/consumer segment, channel | Value chain + consumer → engineering/market |
| Where does money leak? | What is the gross flow, avoidable share, addressable share, beneficiary/cost bearer, and counterfactual? | Trade/value data, material balances, loss studies, cost/time evidence | Flow, location, stage, season, currency/price year | Trade + modeler → Skeptic |
| What could we build? | What recurring need, buyer, willingness to pay, capability, operating model, competition and build path exist? | Buyer behavior, provider mapping, specifications, regulations, input/infrastructure evidence | Discovery mode, geography, customer, scale | Discovery → specialists → Skeptic |
| What infrastructure unlocks multiple industries? | What service level is missing, for whom, what can be shared, and how is capacity governed/priced? | Reliability/utilization, user demand, technical options, regulation, lifecycle cost | Asset/corridor/hub, user industry, time profile | Infrastructure → engineer/modeler |
| What should we not build? | Which thesis fails and under what demand, technical, safety, regulatory, cost, scale or opportunity-cost condition? | Counterevidence, failed precedents where traceable, downside tests, alternatives | Candidate and scenario | Skeptic → Director/human |

## Cross-cutting question families

* **Consumer/product:** measured cleanliness/impurities, consistency, aroma, cooking/taste, price/value, availability and repeat choice versus pack durability, convenience, graphic cues, trust and recognition.
* **Process:** quantity, quality, time, loss, ownership, capacity/utilization, bottleneck, utility and control at every chain step.
* **Machinery:** required function/duty, purchase/assembly/fabrication/redesign path, lifecycle support, utilities, spares, skills, downtime and failure modes.
* **Circularity:** composition, safety, volume, season/location, current disposition, potential users, specification gap, treatment, logistics, regulation and economics for every stream.
* **Wealth:** wages, farmer-income change, local supplier expenditure, employee participation, African investor returns, retained locally controlled capital, assets, and appropriate public revenues—separate, attributable and not double-counted.
* **Ownership safeguards:** record only documented organizational/ownership evidence with lawful provenance; never infer personal identity or use identity as automated screening.
