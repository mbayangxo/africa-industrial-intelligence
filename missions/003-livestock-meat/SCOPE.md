# Mission 003 — Livestock & meat (scope only)

**Status:** NOT STARTED — foundation placeholder.

## Future questions

Map Fulani/pastoral and other supplier networks without treating communities as homogeneous; productivity; forage/seeds, nutrition, water/irrigation, veterinary/vaccination/mobile clinic systems, identification/traceability, aggregation, fair transparent purchasing, transport and welfare.

Assess humane slaughter, processing, safety, cold chain, frozen and ready-to-cook/dibi/prepared African foods, restaurant/institutional bulk supply, domestic/intra-African demand, and global export only where legally and economically viable.

Map regulated uses or disposal pathways for hides, bones, fats, safe offal, manure/biogas, and other outputs. Include disease, public-health, environmental, cultural, land/grazing, seasonal, ownership, and livelihood constraints.
