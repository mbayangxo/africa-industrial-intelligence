# Mission 005 — Consumer brand / packaging intelligence (scope only)

**Status:** NOT STARTED — foundation placeholder. No brand winner has been selected.

## Future questions

Why consumers choose imported versus local products: actual quality, consistency, cleanliness, taste/smell, materials, print/typography/color, trust, tamper evidence, convenience/resealing/carrying/storage, sizes, certifications, prestige, retail placement, price, advertising, and availability. Compare behavior and product performance, not nationality sentiment alone.

**Principle:** BETTER INSIDE AND BETTER OUTSIDE. Products should compete with or exceed strong imports.
